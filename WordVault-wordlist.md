# WordVault — the word list

**1158 crypto terms** and the official **BIP-39 2,048-word seed list**. Every seed word is a word real wallets use to generate recovery phrases. Entries marked ● also have a full dictionary definition in WordVault.html.

## Crypto terms A–Z

### 2017 ICO Boom  ·  History  ·  Advanced
The mania in which thousands of tokens raised billions in ether, most without a product. It gave crypto its first bubble vocabulary, and the SEC its first enforcement wave.

### 2018 Crypto Winter  ·  History  ·  Advanced
The brutal year after the boom, when Bitcoin fell about 84% and most 2017 tokens went to zero. Very few of the projects that survived are still relevant.

### 2021 Bull Run  ·  History  ·  Advanced
Bitcoin to $69,000, Ethereum to $4,800, NFT mania, and the celebrity token wave. It ended with the collapse of Luna instead.

### 24h Volume  ·  Metrics  ·  Intermediate
Trading value over the last day. Compare it to market cap to judge how actively a coin changes hands.

### 2FA  ·  Security  ·  Basic
Two-Factor Authentication - a second step to log in. Use an authenticator app or hardware key, not SMS.

### 51% Attack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
When one group controls more than half of a network's mining or staking power and can reverse recent transactions or double-spend.

### Aave  ·  Names  ·  Intermediate
The biggest DeFi lending protocol, running across Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum and more, and the standard for on-chain credit markets.

### ABI  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Application Binary Interface - the JSON description that tells a wallet or website how to encode calls to a smart contract.

### Account  ·  Ethereum  ·  Advanced
Every address on Ethereum is an account. It is either a wallet you control (EOA) or a smart contract.

### Account Abstraction  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
ERC-4337 - making smart contract wallets first-class citizens, so you get gasless transactions, social recovery, spending limits and passkeys.

### Account Recovery  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
Getting back into a wallet after losing access - only possible with backups, guardians or a custodial service you trusted.

### Accredited Investor  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
A US investor who meets income or wealth thresholds and can access private offerings that ordinary investors cannot.

### Activation  ·  Basics  ·  Advanced
The moment a protocol upgrade switches on at a specific block height or time.

### Active Addresses  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
How many unique wallets transacted in a period. Useful, though bots and airdrop hunters inflate it.

### Active Supply  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
The share of coins that have moved within a given period - a measure of how much of the float is genuinely circulating.

### Address  ·  Security  ·  Basic
The public destination for funds that looks like jumble - 0x71C... on Ethereum, bc1q... on Bitcoin. Safe to share, not safe to mistype.

### Address Poisoning  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Sending a tiny amount from a lookalike address so it appears in your history and you copy it next time you send a large payment.

### Admin Key  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
The key that can change a protocol's contracts. If it is held by one person, the protocol is not really decentralised yet.

### Aerodrome  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The dominant automated market maker and vote-escrow hub on Base, modelled on Velodrome and capturing most of the chain's trading fees.

### Agent Network  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
The human cash-in, cash-out layer - kiosks and agents who convert digital balances to physical notes. Invisible to crypto Twitter, essential in practice.

### Agent Payments  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
The rails letting AI systems pay per call in stablecoins - the intersection of crypto, APIs and machine commerce.

### Agent Reputation  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
On-chain history that lets you judge whether an agent has behaved well before you let it touch your funds.

### Agentic Commerce  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
Machines buying from machines - an agent discovering a service, paying for it in stablecoins and using the result, all without human checkout. Roughly 120 million agent transactions were logged in the first quarter of 2026.

### AgentKit  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
Coinbase's toolkit for giving AI agents wallets, letting them pay, trade and transact with explicit spending limits.

### Aggregator  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
A service that splits your trade across many DEXs to get the best overall price - 1inch, Jupiter, Odos.

### AI Agent  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Intermediate
An autonomous program with its own wallet that can research, trade, pay for services and act on-chain without a human clicking approve. The defining narrative of 2025-2026.
_Example: Virtuals and Eliza-style agents now run token economies and pay each other in stablecoins._

### AI Wallet  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
A wallet controlled by an AI agent with spending limits and human guardrails, so the agent can transact but cannot drain everything.

### AI16Z / Eliza  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
The open-source agent framework and its associated token, used by developers to launch autonomous agents with on-chain personalities.

### Air Plane Test  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Testing a backup restore with no network connection, proving your words and your process both work.

### Air-gapped  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A device that has never touched a network, so nothing online can reach it. Achieved with QR codes or SD cards.

### Air-Gapped Signing  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Signing with a device that has never been connected to anything, transferring the transaction by QR code or SD card.

### Airdrop  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Free tokens distributed to users, usually as a reward for past usage or a marketing push for a new network.

### Airdrop Farming  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Systematically using a protocol with multiple wallets to maximise a future token reward. Now a profession with its own tooling.

### Airdrop Scam  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A fake airdrop that links you to a drainer instead of free tokens. If the airdrop is a surprise, it is probably a trap.

### Alameda Research  ·  History  ·  Advanced
SBF's trading firm, given special privileges on FTX and secretly funded with customer assets. Its balance sheet was the smoking gun.

### Algorithmic Stablecoin  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
A stablecoin kept on peg by code and arbitrage rather than reserves. TerraUSD's collapse in 2022 wiped out about $40 billion.

### Allowance  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
The spending limit you have granted a contract for a specific token.

### Allowance Explorer  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A tool listing every contract your wallet has approved to spend tokens, with a one-tap revoke.

### Allowlist  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A pre-approved list of wallets allowed to buy at launch. The polite name for a whitelist.

### Alpha Group  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A private chat promising early information. Some are real, most are paid promotion with extra steps.

### Alt Season  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
A phase when altcoins outperform Bitcoin. Also called altcoin season.

### Alt-DA  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
Using an alternative data-availability layer instead of Ethereum to publish rollup data — cheaper, and one notch less secure.

### Altcoin  ·  Tokens  ·  Basic
Any crypto that is not Bitcoin. Everything from Ethereum to a meme coin nobody has heard of.

### Altcoin Market Cap  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
The total value of everything that is not Bitcoin or Ether - the number people watch to judge whether alt season has started.

### Altcoin Season Index  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
The share of the top 50 coins outperforming Bitcoin over 90 days. Above 75 was once considered the threshold.

### Altseason  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
See Alt Season. Traders ask about it constantly and it arrives late every time.

### AML  ·  Compliance  ·  Intermediate
Anti-Money Laundering - the rules and monitoring systems designed to stop criminal money flowing through financial services.

### AML/KYC Provider  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Firms like Chainalysis or TRM that give exchanges blockchain forensics and screening tools.

### AMM  ·  DeFi  ·  Intermediate
Automated Market Maker - the formula that prices swaps in a pool based on the balance of the assets in it, instead of an order book.

### Anchor  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The most used Solana development framework in Rust, handling account validation and serialisation so developers write less boilerplate.

### Anchor Output  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
A small output added to a Lightning channel transaction so either party can fee-bump the closing transaction later.

### Anon  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
An anonymous participant. Crypto runs on anons, for better and worse.

### Anon Dev  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
A developer with no public identity. Common in crypto, and a standing risk - they can disappear, and some have.

### Anti-CBDC Act  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The US bill prohibiting the Federal Reserve from issuing a CBDC directly to consumers, passed by the House 219-210 in July 2025.

### Ape In  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Buying a token immediately, with no research, because of hype or a chart. Sometimes profitable, usually regretted.

### Appchain  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
A blockchain dedicated to a single application, so it gets its own blockspace and fee market without competing with the whole world.

### Approval  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
Permission you give a contract to spend a token on your behalf. Unlimited approvals are how drainers empty wallets.

### Approval Phishing  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A malicious dApp asks for an unlimited token approval, then drains the token later at its leisure.

### APR  ·  DeFi  ·  Intermediate
Annual Percentage Rate - simple interest per year, ignoring compounding.

### Aptos  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A Move-language layer 1 from former Diem engineers, using Block-STM for parallel execution and a formal-verification-heavy security approach.

### APY  ·  DeFi  ·  Intermediate
Annual Percentage Yield - the return per year including compounding. A 100% APR compounded daily is about 171% APY.

### Arbitrage  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Buying an asset where it is cheap and selling where it is expensive at nearly the same moment, capturing the price gap with little risk.

### Arbitrum  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The biggest optimistic rollup by value locked, with the ARB token governing its DAO.

### Archive Node  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A node that keeps the entire historical state of the chain, not just recent data. Needed by block explorers and analytics tools.

### Archive Pruning  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Deleting old state from a node to save disk space, at the cost of being able to query deep history without an archive node.

### Arkham  ·  Data  ·  Advanced
The on-chain intelligence platform that de-anonymised wallets behind a token incentive, sparking a privacy debate in 2024-25.

### Ask  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
The lowest price a seller is currently willing to accept. The gap between bid and ask is the spread.

### ATH  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
All-Time High. Bitcoin's ATH was set in October 2025 above $126,000.

### Atkins  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
Paul Atkins, sworn in as SEC chair in April 2025, who reset SEC crypto policy toward rulemaking, token taxonomy and safe harbours.

### ATL  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
All-Time Low — the lowest price an asset has ever traded at, used as the reference point when people argue about whether a token has bottomed.

### Atomic Swap  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A swap between two parties that either completes fully or not at all, with no counterparty risk, using hash time-locked contracts.

### Attestation  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
A periodic report, often by an accounting firm, confirming that reserves match liabilities. Weaker than a full audit.

### Attestation (Consensus)  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A validator's vote on which chain is correct. Attestations are how proof-of-stake networks accumulate finality.

### Audit  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
An independent review of a smart contract's code by a security firm before launch. Helpful, but audited code still gets hacked.

### Audit Report  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
The written findings of an audit, including every issue discovered and whether it was fixed.

### Authorised Participant  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
The institution allowed to create or redeem ETF shares, keeping supply in line with demand and price close to NAV.

### Auto-Deleveraging  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
ADL - a last-resort measure where an exchange force-closes profitable traders' positions to cover a blown-up account.

### Autonomous Trading Agent  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
A bot that watches data, forms its own view and trades within limits you set. Useful, and capable of losing money extremely fast.

### Avalanche  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
A fast chain built from subnets, used for gaming, institutional deployments and app-specific chains.

### AVS  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Actively Validated Service - a network, oracle or bridge that pays restaked ETH for shared security instead of bootstrapping its own validator set.

### Axie Infinity  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The play-to-earn phenomenon that gave millions in the Philippines and Venezuela a real income, then imploded when token rewards collapsed and the Ronin bridge was hacked for $620 million.

### Babylon  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
A protocol that lets Bitcoin holders stake BTC to help secure proof-of-stake chains, turning the largest dormant asset into a security budget.

### Back-running  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Placing a trade immediately after a large known transaction to profit from the price move it creates.

### Backdoor  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A hidden way into software or a contract that lets an attacker bypass normal controls.

### Backtesting  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Testing a strategy against historical data to see how it would have performed. Beware overfitting.

### Bad Debt  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
A loan that is underwater beyond its collateral, leaving the protocol short. It is the recurring, expensive failure mode of on-chain credit.

### Bag  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Your holdings in a token, especially one that is not doing well. 'Still holding the bag.'

### Bagholder  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Someone left holding a token that has collapsed, still hoping.

### Bagwork  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The unglamorous labour of managing positions, monitoring unlocks and taking profit without emotion.

### Bandwidth Mining  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Earning tokens by sharing your internet connection or location data, as Grass and Helium-style networks do.

### Bank Run  ·  TradFi  ·  Intermediate
When too many depositors withdraw at once. Crypto versions happen on-chain in hours instead of days.

### Bart Pattern  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
A sharp spike up and immediate return down, named for the shape it draws. Usually the footprint of thin liquidity.

### Base  ·  Names  ·  Intermediate
Coinbase's OP Stack layer 2, launched in 2023, now one of the busiest chains for consumer apps and coin launches.

### Base Fee  ·  Ethereum  ·  Advanced
The algorithmically set minimum fee per unit of gas that EIP-1559 introduced. It rises when blocks are full and is burned, not paid to validators.

### Base Name  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
A human-readable name on Coinbase's Base network, like alice.base.eth, used in place of a hex address.

### Based  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Respectful approval of a bold, honest opinion or action, even if it is unpopular.

### Based Rollup  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
A rollup that hands sequencing back to Ethereum's own block producers, inheriting liveness and a form of decentralisation by default.

### Basis Point  ·  TradFi  ·  Intermediate
One hundredth of a percent - 0.01%. Fees, spreads and yields in crypto are all quoted this way; 100 bps is 1%.

### Basis Trade  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
Buying spot and selling futures at a higher price, capturing the difference at expiry. The dominant institutional crypto strategy.

### Basis Trade (Cash and Carry)  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
Buying spot, selling the future, and pocketing the difference at expiry. Boring, popular, and a main driver of institutional flows.

### Batch Transaction  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Paying many recipients in a single transaction to save fees - standard for exchanges and payroll.

### Beacon Chain  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Ethereum's proof-of-stake coordination layer, launched December 2020, merged with the execution chain in September 2022.

### Bear Market  ·  Trading  ·  Basic
A long stretch of falling prices and pessimism. Crypto winters last around a year on average, sometimes much longer.

### Bear Trap  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
A dip that scares holders into selling right before a sharp rebound.

### Bearish  ·  Trading  ·  Basic
Expecting prices to go down.

### Bech32  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
The address format used by modern Bitcoin addresses, with built-in error detection and lowercase-only rules.

### Believe  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A 2026-era launch platform that pushed social-token launches onto Solana, letting creators mint a coin from a post.

### BEP-20  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The BNB Smart Chain token standard, compatible with the tooling built for ERC-20.

### Berachain  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A proof-of-liquidity chain where validators must provide liquidity to the ecosystem, with a three-token model governing gas, staking and governance.

### Bid  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
The highest price a buyer is currently willing to pay.

### Binance  ·  Names  ·  Basic
The largest global exchange. Founded by CZ, banned from the US market, still dominant everywhere else, and the platform most retail users worldwide touch first.

### Binary Market  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
A prediction market with only two outcomes, where a YES share pays $1 if the event happens and nothing otherwise.

### BIP-32  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
The standard that lets one seed phrase generate an entire tree of keys, one for each account and address.

### BIP-39  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
The 2013 standard that defines the 2,048-word list - abandon, ability, able... zone, zoo - that seed phrases are drawn from, plus the checksum that catches typos.
_Example: A 12-word phrase has 128 bits of entropy; a 24-word phrase has 256._

### BIP-44  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
The convention that puts order on that tree, giving every coin a path like m/44'/0'/0'/0/0 so any wallet can restore the same addresses.

### Bitcoin  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Basic
The first cryptocurrency, launched in 2009 by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto. A fixed supply of 21 million coins, secured by proof of work, designed as peer-to-peer electronic cash.

### Bitcoin Cash  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The 2017 hard fork that raised Bitcoin's block size for cheaper payments.

### Bitcoin Cash Fork  ·  History  ·  Advanced
The August 2017 chain split over block size, when Bitcoin Cash raised the block limit to 8MB and began its own chain.

### Bitcoin Core  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Intermediate
The reference open-source software that most Bitcoin nodes run, maintained by a global group of volunteer developers.

### Bitcoin Dominance  ·  Metrics  ·  Intermediate
Bitcoin's share of the total crypto market value. When it rises, money is usually rotating out of altcoins into BTC.

### Bitcoin Ordinals Boom  ·  History  ·  Advanced
Early 2023, when inscriptions filled Bitcoin blocks for the first time. Fees spiked, purists fumed, and a new sub-economy was born.

### Bitcoin Season  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
A stretch when BTC outperforms the rest of the market, the opposite of alt season.

### Bitcoin Whitepaper  ·  History  ·  Intermediate
Nine pages published to a cryptography mailing list on 31 October 2008 by Satoshi Nakamoto, titled Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System. It proposed solving double-spending without a trusted third party.

### BitLicense  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
New York's state crypto licence, introduced in 2015. Expensive and slow, and many firms simply left New York instead.

### Bittensor  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
A network of competing machine-learning subnets where models earn TAO for producing the best outputs — the closest thing to an on-chain AI marketplace.

### Black Hat  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A hacker who exploits vulnerabilities for personal gain.

### BlackRock Bitcoin ETF  ·  History  ·  Advanced
IBIT, the iShares spot Bitcoin ETF. It became the fastest-growing ETF in history and now holds more coins than most governments.

### Blind Signing  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Approving a transaction you cannot read. It is how most hardware wallet victims lose funds; simulate or refuse.

### Blinks  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Solana's blockchain links — a normal URL that turns a transaction into a shareable button inside any website or social post.

### Blob  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
A temporary, cheap chunk of data that layer 2s post to Ethereum. Blobs made L2 fees collapse when EIP-4844 launched in March 2024.

### Block  ·  Basics  ·  Basic
A bundle of transactions that gets added to the chain as one unit, stamped with a timestamp and a reference to the previous block.

### Block Builder  ·  Ethereum  ·  Advanced
A specialized actor that assembles the most profitable possible block from a bundle of transactions and sells it to a validator.

### Block Explorer  ·  Tech  ·  Basic
The search engine for a blockchain. Paste an address, transaction or block and see everything.

### Block Reward  ·  Mining  ·  Intermediate
The new coins a miner or validator receives for producing a valid block, plus the fees inside it.

### Block Space  ·  Tech  ·  Intermediate
The limited capacity inside each block. Fees are simply the auction price for that space.

### Block Time  ·  Tech  ·  Intermediate
How often a chain produces a new block - ten minutes on Bitcoin, twelve seconds on Ethereum, about 400 milliseconds on Solana.

### Blockchain  ·  Basics  ·  Basic
A shared, append-only ledger of transactions copied across thousands of computers, where every block of data is cryptographically locked to the one before it. Nobody can quietly rewrite history.
_Example: Bitcoin runs on the first and most famous blockchain._

### Blood in the Streets  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A favourite saying for buying when everything is collapsing. Borrowed from markets much older than crypto.

### Blur  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The pro-trader NFT marketplace that turned NFT trading into a points and bidding game and dominated volume from 2023.

### BNB  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
Binance's exchange token, used for fee discounts and as gas on BNB Smart Chain.

### Bonding Curve  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
A formula that sets a token's price as a function of its supply, so early buyers pay less and the price rises automatically as people buy.

### Bonk  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The Solana dog coin that powered the 2023-24 Solana revival and airdropped huge sums to users.

### BONKbot  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The Telegram trading bot that made fast Solana sniping mainstream among retail traders.

### Borrowing  ·  DeFi  ·  Intermediate
Taking a loan against collateral in a smart contract. No credit check, no forms, but you must stay above the collateral ratio.

### Bought the Top  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The painful experience of buying at the exact peak. Everyone has their own top.

### Bounty  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A reward paid for finding problems - or in old-school scams, the promise nobody ever actually collects.

### Brain Wallet  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A wallet whose key is derived from a passphrase you memorised. Almost always a terrible idea - attackers brute force famous phrases instantly.

### BRC-20  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
An experimental fungible token standard on Bitcoin that uses Ordinals inscriptions to create and transfer tokens. Clunky, but it kicked off Bitcoin's meme-token wave.

### Brian Armstrong  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
Coinbase's co-founder and CEO, and the most prominent public voice for US crypto policy.

### Bribes (veTokens)  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Payments made to veToken holders to direct emissions toward a specific pool. Curve's bribe market became a whole sub-economy.

### Bridge  ·  Tech  ·  Intermediate
A protocol that moves assets between chains by locking them on one side and issuing a representation on the other. Bridges are the single most hacked category in crypto.

### Bridge Hack  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
The category of attack that has stolen more money than any other. Bridges hold locked assets in one place, which makes them the richest single target in crypto.

### Broadcast  ·  Basics  ·  Intermediate
Pushing a signed transaction out to the peer-to-peer network so nodes and miners can see it.

### BSTX / Bank Custody  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Banks re-entering crypto custody after the 2025 repeal of SAB 121 made holding client coins balance-sheet neutral.

### BTC  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Basic
The ticker symbol for one bitcoin. It splits into 100 million smaller units called satoshis.

### BTC Dominance Break  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
The point where Bitcoin's share of the market falls sharply, the classic signal traders wait for before declaring alt season.

### Bug Bounty  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A standing reward programme for white hats who report vulnerabilities instead of exploiting them. Immunefi hosts the largest crypto ones.

### Bull Market  ·  Trading  ·  Basic
A long stretch of rising prices, optimism and new money arriving. Everything looks like genius.

### Bull Trap  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
A rally that lures buyers in before the price collapses again.

### Bullish  ·  Trading  ·  Basic
Expecting prices to go up. Bullish on ETH means you think ETH rises.

### Burn  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
Sending tokens to an unspendable address, permanently removing them from supply - usually to create scarcity or offset inflation.

### Burner Email  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A throwaway email used for crypto sign-ups so a data breach never links your identity to a wallet.

### Burner Wallet  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
A throwaway wallet used for risky mints and unknown sites, holding only what you can afford to lose.

### Buy the Dip  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Buying after a price drop. Easy to say, harder to do, and lethal with leverage.

### Buyback and Burn  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
Using protocol revenue to buy tokens on the market and destroy them, mimicking a share buyback to reward holders.

### Buyback Program  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
A protocol using its revenue or treasury to purchase its own token on the open market.

### Bybit  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
A derivatives-heavy exchange popular in Asia and among professional traders. It suffered a record $1.5 billion hack in February 2025.

### Bybit Hack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
February 2025 - about $1.5 billion in Ether was taken from Bybit's cold wallet through a compromised multisig interface, the largest crypto theft ever recorded.

### Bytecode  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The compiled, machine-readable version of a smart contract that actually runs on the EVM or an SVM.

### Byzantine Fault Tolerance  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The ability of a network to keep working correctly even when some participants lie, cheat or go offline - the core problem blockchains solve.

### Cabal  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Accusation aimed at anyone who bought before you did. Occasionally true, usually projection.

### Cairo  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Starknet's programming language, designed so programs can be proven with STARKs.

### Calldata  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The data an Ethereum transaction carries, and pre-blob the main cost of posting rollup batches to L1.

### Canonical Bridge  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
The official bridge a rollup uses to move its own assets to and from its base chain.

### Capital Controls  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Government limits on moving money across borders. They are the single biggest reason stablecoin adoption explodes in a country.

### Capital Rotation  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Money moving between sectors — BTC to majors, majors to alts, alts to memecoins, then back out to stablecoins.

### Capitulate  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
To surrender - the moment you sell everything because you cannot take the pain any more.

### Capitulation  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
The point where holders give up and sell at a loss. It often marks the bottom, which is infuriating.

### Card Programme  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
A crypto debit card that spends from a stablecoin balance through a traditional card network.

### Cardano  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The research-driven proof-of-stake chain founded by Charles Hoskinson, known for meticulous formal methods.

### Carry Trade  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
Borrowing where interest is cheap and earning where it is expensive. In crypto, the funding-rate version is one of the most crowded trades in the market.

### Cascading Liquidations  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
A chain reaction where forced sells push the price down, triggering more forced sells. This is what makes leverage so dangerous.

### Cash-in  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
Turning physical cash into a digital balance, whether mobile money or crypto. Still the hardest part of adoption in the real world.

### Cash-out  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
Turning a digital balance back into physical notes. Liquidity at this step decides whether crypto is actually usable day to day.

### CBDC  ·  Regulation  ·  Intermediate
Central Bank Digital Currency - a digital currency issued directly by a central bank. The US Anti-CBDC Act passed the House in 2025 and would prohibit the Federal Reserve from offering one to individuals.

### CBRT  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Turkey's central bank, which bans crypto payments while millions of Turks use stablecoins to protect savings from lira inflation.

### CDP  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Collateralized Debt Position - minting a stablecoin against locked collateral, as in MakerDAO's DAI.

### CDP Vault  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
See CDP - in MakerDAO you lock collateral and mint DAI, paying a stability fee.

### Celestia  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
The first modular data-availability chain, letting rollups publish their data somewhere far cheaper than Ethereum.

### Celsius Collapse  ·  History  ·  Advanced
A lending platform that promised high yields on deposits, froze withdrawals in June 2022 and filed for bankruptcy. Its founder was later charged with fraud.

### CEX  ·  Trading  ·  Basic
Centralized Exchange - Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX. Fast, liquid and easy, but you are trusting a company with custody.

### CFT  ·  Compliance  ·  Advanced
Countering the Financing of Terrorism - the sister set of rules to AML, focused on terrorist financing.

### CFTC  ·  Regulation  ·  Intermediate
The US Commodity Futures Trading Commission, which regulates derivatives - and is positioned to oversee most crypto spot markets for assets classified as digital commodities.

### Chain Abstraction  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Making the underlying chain invisible to users so they can hold funds anywhere and pay for anything without thinking about bridges or gas tokens.

### Proof of Stake  ·  Mining  ·  Basic
A consensus method where validators lock up coins as collateral. Misbehave and your stake gets slashed; behave and you earn rewards. Ethereum, Solana and Cardano use it.

### Proof of Work  ·  Mining  ·  Basic
A consensus method where miners burn electricity to prove they did real work, making it costly to attack the network. Bitcoin and Litecoin use it.

### Proposer-Builder Separation  ·  Ethereum  ·  Advanced
PBS - splitting the job of choosing transactions from the job of proposing blocks, so that vanilla validators do not need to be MEV experts.

### Protocol  ·  Basics  ·  Intermediate
The formal set of rules that defines how a network behaves - how blocks form, how nodes talk and what counts as valid.

### Protocol Revenue  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
Fees retained by a protocol rather than paid to validators or liquidity providers. The number that justifies a token's existence.

### Provenance  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The verifiable history of an NFT or token - who made it, who held it, when it moved. Impossible to fake retroactively.

### Proxy Contract  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A pattern where the upgradeable logic lives in one contract and your funds and state live in another, so the logic can be replaced without moving the assets.

### Proxy Upgrade Attack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Taking over the admin key of an upgradeable contract and pointing it at a malicious implementation.

### PSBT  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
Partially Signed Bitcoin Transaction - a standard file that lets one device or person add signatures and another broadcast, which is how multisig and hardware wallets cooperate.

### Public Address Hygiene  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Keeping track of which addresses you have made public, and never mixing your main wallet into those conversations.

### Public Key  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
The cryptographic partner to a private key. It can be shared freely and is used to derive your address and verify your signatures.

### Puell Multiple  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
Miner revenue compared with its yearly average, used to spot when miners are over-earning and likely to sell.

### Pump  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A sharp move up in price, sometimes orchestrated.

### Pump and Dump  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Hype a coin artificially, sell into the buyers, leave them holding. Illegal in regulated markets, routine in meme tokens.

### Pump.fun  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The bonding-curve launchpad that let anyone create a Solana memecoin in seconds. It industrialised speculation and reshaped 2024-2026 culture.

### Pyramid Scheme  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
A scheme that mostly pays recruitment commissions rather than real product revenue.

### Qualified Custodian  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
A custodian that meets specific regulatory standards, required for many institutional and ETF holdings.

### Quorum  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
The minimum number of votes a proposal needs to be valid. Without quorum, even a majority cannot force a change.

### R Multiple  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Profit or loss expressed as a multiple of the risk taken — 2R means you made twice what you risked.

### Ransomware  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Malware that encrypts your files and demands crypto to unlock them.

### Rarity  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
How uncommon an item's combination of traits is. Rarity tools score every NFT against its collection.

### Raydium  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The main Solana AMM and launch venue where Pump.fun graduates trade.

### RBF  ·  Basics  ·  Advanced
Replace-By-Fee - replacing an unconfirmed Bitcoin transaction with a new one paying a higher fee to get it mined.

### RBI  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The Reserve Bank of India, which taxes crypto gains at 30% plus a 1% transaction tax and has historically discouraged banks from serving crypto firms.

### Real Yield  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Yield paid from actual protocol revenue in a stable asset, not from printing new tokens.

### Realised Cap  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
A valuation that prices every coin at the last time it moved, rather than at today's price. It approximates how much money is actually in Bitcoin.

### Rebalancing  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
Bringing a portfolio back to target weights — mechanically selling what ran and buying what lagged.

### Recognition vs Recall  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Being able to spot a word is easier than producing it from memory. Seed phrases need recognition and spelling, not deep understanding.

### Recovery Kit  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
The physical package holding your backups, the derivation path, the wallet software name and clear instructions for your family.

### Recovery Phrase Storage  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
How you keep the words: paper, steel, split across locations - and never in a phone, photo, cloud note or email draft.

### Recovery Scam  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Someone offers to recover funds you already lost - the second robbery, usually aimed at the same victims.

### Recursive Borrowing  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Borrowing against collateral, depositing the borrowed asset as new collateral, and repeating to build leveraged exposure. Efficient, and the mechanism behind many liquidation cascades.

### Redemption  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
Exchanging a token back for the asset it represents, for example burning USDC for dollars.

### Redemption Window  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
The period in which fund shares can be exchanged for the underlying asset, which is what keeps an ETF price tethered to its NAV.

### Reentrancy Attack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
When a contract calls out to an attacker's contract before updating its own balances, letting the attacker re-enter and drain it. The 2016 DAO hack.

### Reflexivity  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
The loop where price moves change the story, which changes behaviour, which moves the price. Soros's term, and crypto's daily reality.

### Registered Exchange  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
An exchange licensed by a national regulator, required to separate customer funds, publish reserves and follow AML rules.

### RegTech  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Software that automates compliance - monitoring, screening and reporting for crypto businesses.

### Regulation S  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The US exemption covering offers made to investors outside the United States.

### Regulatory Arbitrage  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Choosing a jurisdiction because the rules are easier. A founding habit of the industry, now losing its power as rules converge.

### Regulatory Clarity  ·  Regulation  ·  Intermediate
The thing the entire industry says it wants: rules that say what is allowed before you build it, instead of lawsuits after.

### Regulatory Sandbox  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
A supervised testing ground where startups can offer new crypto products to limited users without full licensing.

### Rekt  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Wrecked - badly losing money on a trade, a hack or a rug.

### Rektproof  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A portfolio built so that no single mistake can end it. The grown-up version of diamond hands.

### Remittance  ·  TradFi  ·  Intermediate
Money sent home across borders. Stablecoins have cut the cost and time dramatically - minutes instead of days, and often a fraction of the fee.

### Remittance Corridor  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
A specific sending route, such as UK to Nigeria or US to the Philippines. Corridors with weak banking are where stablecoins win fastest.

### Renounce Ownership  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Voluntarily giving up the admin key so nobody, including the team, can ever change the contract again.

### Renounce Ownership (Safe)  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Handing the admin key to a burn address. Genuinely safer than a live key, though it cannot be undone.

### Renounced  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
When a team gives up control of the contract's dangerous powers. Good for safety, though renounced contracts can still be bad projects.

### Replay Attack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Re-broadcasting a valid signed transaction on another chain or in another context to trick a system into acting twice.

### Repurchase Agreement  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
Repo - short-term borrowing secured by collateral. On-chain versions are growing, with tokenised treasuries as collateral.

### Reserve  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
The assets held to back issued tokens - T-bills, cash or crypto. Under the US GENIUS Act, payment stablecoin issuers must hold one-to-one reserves.

### Reserve Factor  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The cut of borrower interest a protocol keeps for its treasury and insurance.

### Reserve Requirement  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Under the GENIUS Act, stablecoin issuers must hold assets equal to at least the face value of tokens outstanding, in cash or short-term Treasuries.

### Restaking  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Re-using already staked coins to secure additional networks at the same time, earning extra yield in exchange for extra slashing risk.

### Restore Test  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
Restoring an empty or test wallet from your backup to prove the backup actually works, before you need it.

### Reth  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A fast Ethereum execution client written in Rust by Paradigm, known for aggressive performance work and modular components.

### Retroactive Airdrop  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Rewarding past users who had no idea they were being tracked. It created an entire industry of people using protocols purely to farm future tokens.

### Reverse Solicitation  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The legal grey area where a foreign crypto firm serves a country's residents because they came to it first. Regulators increasingly reject the defence.

### Revert  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
When a transaction fails on-chain and all its effects are rolled back, though the gas is still spent.

### Revert Protection  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Simulating a transaction before you sign so you never pay gas to watch it fail.

### Revoke  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
Withdrawing a token approval you granted to a contract, cutting off its ability to move your funds in future.

### Revoke.cash  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A tool for listing and revoking every token approval your wallet has granted.

### Ring Signature  ·  Privacy  ·  Advanced
A signature that proves one member of a group signed without revealing which one. The basis of Monero's privacy.

### Ripple  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The company behind XRP and RippleNet, which sells payment software to banks and now issues its own stablecoin, RLUSD.

### RippleNet  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
Ripple's payment messaging network used by banks and remittance firms.

### Risk Budget  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Deciding in advance how much you can lose on a position before you take it. The difference between trading and gambling.

### Roadmap  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
The plan a project publishes. Delivering it is the part that matters.

### Rollup  ·  Scaling  ·  Intermediate
A layer 2 that executes transactions off-chain in batches and publishes compressed data plus a proof back to the main chain.

### Rollup-as-a-Service  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
A provider that spins up a custom rollup for you in a few clicks - chains like Conduit, Caldera and Gelato.

### Romance Scam  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
A relationship built entirely to set up an investment or wallet theft. The crypto version usually ends with a fake exchange and a small withdrawal to prove it works.

### Ronin Bridge Hack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
March 2022, North Korea's Lazarus Group stole about $620 million from Axie Infinity's bridge by compromising validator keys. It bankrupted portions of the ecosystem.

### Rotation  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Money moving from one sector or asset to another - from BTC to alts, or from DeFi to memecoins.

### Router  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The contract that finds the best path for your swap across multiple pools and executes it.

### Royalties  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The creator's cut on secondary sales, usually 2.5-10%. Marketplaces now make them optional for buyers, which enraged creators.

### RPC  ·  Tech  ·  Intermediate
Remote Procedure Call - the endpoint your wallet uses to read the chain and broadcast transactions. If it is down, your wallet looks broken.

### RPC Provider  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A company running blockchain nodes for wallets and dApps - Alchemy, Infura, QuickNode. Centralised in practice, which is an uncomfortable fact.

### RPC Rate Limit  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The request ceiling a provider puts on your wallet or dApp. Hitting it is why a busy app suddenly starts failing.

### Rug Pull  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
When a project's team drains the liquidity or dumps their own supply and disappears, leaving buyers with worthless tokens.

### Rug Signal  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The small early warning signs — unlocked liquidity, a dev wallet dumping, a renamed contract.

### Rugcheck  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
The habit of checking a new token's contract for mint authority, freeze authority, unlocked liquidity and concentrated holders before buying.

### Runes  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
A more efficient Bitcoin token standard launched alongside the 2024 halving, designed to avoid clogging the chain the way BRC-20 did.

### Runway  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
How many months a protocol's treasury can fund its spending. The most honest metric a DAO publishes about itself.

### Rust  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The systems language used by Solana, Polkadot and Near for high-performance on-chain programs.

### RWA  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
Real-World Asset - tokenised treasuries, private credit, real estate, commodities or fund shares living on a blockchain. One of the biggest 2026 narratives.

### SAB 121  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The US accounting rule that forced banks to put custodied crypto on their own balance sheets as a liability. Its repeal in 2025 (via SAB 122) unblocked US bank custody at scale.

### Safe Harbour  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
A legal provision protecting an actor who follows certain conditions. Crypto builders have long asked for one to launch tokens without immediate Securities Law exposure.

### Sam Bankman-Fried  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
SBF - founder of FTX, convicted in 2023 of fraud and sentenced to 25 years for looting customer funds.

### Sanctions Screening  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Checking every wallet you interact with against sanctions lists - now standard at exchanges and increasingly at well-run DeFi front ends.

### Sandwich Attack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A bot spots your pending swap, buys just before you and sells just after, skimming profit from the price move your trade caused.

### Satoshi  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Basic
The smallest unit of bitcoin - 0.00000001 BTC, named after Bitcoin's creator. Stacking sats means buying small amounts regularly.

### Satoshi Nakamoto  ·  Names  ·  Basic
The unknown creator (or creators) of Bitcoin who published the whitepaper in October 2008 and disappeared in 2011, leaving behind roughly one million untouched coins.

### Scale Out  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Selling fractions of a position into strength instead of guessing one perfect exit.

### Scalping  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Taking many tiny profits from small price moves within minutes or seconds.

### Scam  ·  Security  ·  Basic
Anything designed to take your money through deception. In crypto, assume every unsolicited message is a scam until proven otherwise.

### Schnorr Aggregation  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
Combining several signatures into one, so a multisig spend looks like an ordinary single-signature payment on-chain.

### Schnorr Signature  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A newer signature scheme adopted by Bitcoin's Taproot upgrade that is smaller, cheaper to verify and supports key aggregation.

### Scholarship  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
Lending an NFT to a player who cannot afford one, in exchange for a cut of the earnings.

### Searcher  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A bot operator who hunts for profitable MEV opportunities such as arbitrage and liquidations and bids to include them.

### Seat at the Table  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Owning a share of a protocol's governance or revenue rather than just trading its token.

### SEC  ·  Regulation  ·  Intermediate
The US Securities and Exchange Commission, which regulates investments that count as securities. Under Chairman Atkins in 2025-26 it shifted to a friendlier posture, with token taxonomy and safe-harbour proposals replacing enforcement cases.

### SEC Nigeria  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Nigeria's securities regulator, which licenses digital asset exchanges and runs the Accelerated Regulatory Incubation Programme for crypto firms.

### Secure Element  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
The tamper-resistant chip inside hardware wallets that protects keys even if the device is physically attacked.

### Securities Law  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The rules, including the Howey Test in the US, that decide whether something is an investment contract. Most 2017-era token sales failed it.

### Securities Lending  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
Borrowing shares or tokens to short them, paid for with interest. In DeFi it happens automatically through lending pools.

### Seed Phrase  ·  Security  ·  Basic
12 or 24 random words that generate every key in your wallet. Whoever has the words owns the wallet. Back it up offline and never, ever share it.

### Seed Phrase Generator Scam  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
A website offering a free wallet where it already knows every phrase it produces. The crypto equivalent of a rigged deck.

### Seedless Wallet  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A wallet with no phrase to lose, using passkeys, MPC or social recovery instead. Better UX, different trade-offs.

### Seeker  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
Solana Mobile's second phone, shipping with a built-in crypto wallet, a dApp store and a token airdrop to owners.

### SegWit  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
Segregated Witness - a 2017 upgrade that moved signature data out of the main block, fixing transaction malleability and raising capacity.

### Sei  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A trading-focused chain that added parallel EVM execution to serve order-book and derivatives apps.

### Selective Disclosure  ·  Privacy  ·  Advanced
Revealing only the specific facts a counterparty needs - you earn over a threshold, you are not sanctioned - and nothing else.

### Self-Custody  ·  Security  ·  Basic
Holding your own keys, which means holding your own risk. Not your keys, not your coins.

### Self-Regulatory Organization  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
An industry body with delegated rulemaking power. Several have been proposed for crypto, none has stuck.

### SELFDESTRUCT  ·  Ethereum  ·  Advanced
The opcode that deletes a contract and returns its balance. It was heavily restricted because it complicated state proofs.

### Sequencer  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
The node on an L2 that orders and executes your transactions before batching them to L1. If it goes down, the chain stalls until it returns.

### Ser  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Crypto Twitter spelling of 'sir', used ironically or warmly. 'Ser, this is a Wendy's.'

### Session Key  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A temporary key with restricted permissions, used in games and trading bots so the main key never leaves cold storage.

### Settlement  ·  TradFi  ·  Intermediate
The final step where assets actually change hands and the transfer becomes irreversible. Crypto settles in seconds to minutes; banks take days.

### Settlement Layer  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
The chain where final, irreversible ownership transfer happens, as opposed to the faster layers where trades are executed.

### Settlement Without Admission  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Paying a fine without admitting wrongdoing, the most common ending for crypto enforcement actions.

### Sextortion Scam  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Emails claiming to know your secrets and threatening exposure unless you send BTC. Delete and ignore.

### SFC Hong Kong  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission, which licenses virtual asset trading platforms and in 2024 approved spot bitcoin and ether ETFs for the city.

### SFP  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Swing failure pattern — a wick above resistance that immediately reverses, often trapping breakout buyers.

### SHA-256  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The hash function at the heart of Bitcoin. It turns any input into a unique 256-bit fingerprint.

### Shamir Backup  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Mathematically splitting a seed into shares that only reconstruct when enough are combined.

### Shard Chain  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
One of the parallel partitions in a sharded design. Ethereum eventually chose rollups plus data blobs over execution sharding.

### Sharding  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Splitting a chain's workload across many parallel groups of nodes so throughput scales with the network, rather than being capped by every node doing everything.

### Shared Security  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Borrowing the security of a big chain instead of building your own validator set from scratch.

### Sharpe Ratio  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Return per unit of volatility. Useful, easily gamed, and always better quoted over multi-year windows.

### Shiba Inu  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The dog-themed token that followed Dogecoin and built a whole ecosystem around the meme.

### Shill  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Aggressively promoting a project, often because you own it or are paid to. Objectivity not included.

### Shiller  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A person whose main contribution is promotion, usually of their own bags.

### Shitcoin  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
A crypto asset with no real use case, no credible team and no reason to exist beyond speculation. Sometimes affectionate, sometimes accurate.

### Short  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
Betting the price will fall, usually by borrowing the asset and selling it, hoping to buy it back cheaper.

### Sidechain  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
A separate blockchain connected to a main chain by a bridge, with its own validators and security. Faster, but less secure than a true rollup.

### Signature  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
The cryptographic proof attached to a transaction showing it came from the owner of the private key.

### Signature Phishing  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
You never approve anything on-chain — you just sign a message that grants total access. The most common high-value theft in crypto.

### Signing  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
Producing a cryptographic signature with your private key that proves you authorised a transaction, without revealing the key itself.

### Silk Road  ·  History  ·  Advanced
The darknet marketplace that ran on Bitcoin from 2011 to 2013 and gave the world its first mass criminal case for crypto. The FBI seized 144,000 BTC from Ross Ulbricht.

### SIM Swap  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
An attacker takes over your phone number to intercept SMS codes and reset your accounts. It is why SMS-based 2FA is discouraged.

### Simulation API  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A service that shows the exact asset changes a transaction will cause - the foundation of every modern wallet's safety warnings.

### Simulation Preview  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A wallet feature showing what a transaction will actually do to your balances - the single best defence against drainers.

### Sky  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The protocol formerly known as MakerDAO, which rebranded in 2024 and issues the USDS stablecoin.

### Slashing  ·  Mining  ·  Intermediate
The penalty that burns or redistributes a validator's stake for breaking protocol rules or going offline.

### Slippage  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
The difference between the price you expected and the price you actually got. Worse in thin pools and big orders.

### Slippage (Pool)  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
In an AMM, price impact and slippage both grow with order size relative to pool depth.

### Slippage Budget  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
The maximum worsening of price a trading desk will tolerate on an order. Instinctively, this is what slippage tolerance does on a DEX.

### Slippage Tolerance  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The maximum slippage you will accept on a DEX trade. Too tight and it fails; too loose and a sandwich bot eats you.

### Slot  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A fixed time window in which one validator may propose a block - twelve seconds on Ethereum, about 400 milliseconds on Solana.

### Smart Account  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A wallet that is itself a smart contract, so it can enforce rules - two-factor approval, daily limits, automatic payments.

### Smart Contract  ·  Tech  ·  Basic
Self-executing code deployed to a blockchain that runs exactly as written, with no human able to change the outcome mid-flight.

### Smart Money  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Wallets with good track records. Following them is informative but never a guarantee.

### Smartphone Wallet  ·  Security  ·  Basic
A self-custody wallet on the phone in your pocket - the most common form of crypto ownership in the world outside the US and Europe.

### Snapshot  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The exact moment balances are recorded for an airdrop. After it, moving your tokens changes nothing.

### Sneak Peek  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Early access to a launch given to insiders — the mechanism behind most unfair distributions.

### Social Engineering  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
Talking people into breaking their own security. It is now the most effective attack in crypto, ahead of any code exploit.

### Social Login Wallet  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A wallet created with an email or Google account, with the key held in an enclave or split across shares. Superb UX, custodial-adjacent trust.

### Social Recovery  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Splitting recovery power among guardians you trust, so losing your phone does not mean losing your funds.

### SocialFi  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
Social apps with tokens attached - tipping, creator coins and revenue sharing built into the feed.

### Socialised Losses  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Spreading a protocol's shortfall across all depositors rather than the users who caused it.

### Soft Confirmation  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A pre-confirmation that a transaction is likely to land. Fast and useful, but not final.

### Soft Fork  ·  Basics  ·  Advanced
A backwards-compatible rule tightening. Non-upgraded nodes still accept the new blocks, so the chain does not split.
_Example: SegWit was activated on Bitcoin as a soft fork._

### Soft Rug  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A slower exit - the team quietly sells their allocations and stops developing instead of draining the pool.

### Solana  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
The fast, cheap proof-of-history chain that has become home to memecoins, consumer apps and most of the payment stablecoin growth since 2024.

### Solana Actions  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The API behind Blinks: endpoints that return a signable transaction for any client to render.

### Solana Outage Era  ·  History  ·  Advanced
The 2021-2022 stretch when Solana stalled repeatedly under load, the criticism that drove its rebuild into one of the fastest and most reliable chains in production.

### Solidity  ·  Tech  ·  Intermediate
The most widely used programming language for writing Ethereum smart contracts. Its files end in .sol.

### Solscan  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
A block explorer for Solana, and where everyone checks their memecoin transfers.

### Solv  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
A Bitcoin yield protocol issuing SolvBTC, one of several attempts to make BTC productive without giving up custody.

### Solver  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
A network participant that competes to fulfil user intents - finding routes, filling orders and settling them on-chain for a fee.

### SOPR  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
Spent Output Profit Ratio - whether coins moving on-chain are being sold at a profit or a loss. Persistent losses signal capitulation.

### Soros Reflexivity  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
See reflexivity — the reason narratives and prices co-create each other in a market with no earnings anchor.

### Soulbound Token  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
A non-transferable token that proves something about you - attendance, membership, a credential - and cannot be bought or sold.

### Source Code Audit Trail  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Keeping the exact commit, compiler version and settings that produced a deployed contract, so verification is reproducible.

### Spaced Repetition  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Reviewing material at growing intervals so it sticks. The reason flashcards beat re-reading everything twice.

### Spear Phishing  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Phishing aimed at one specific person, using details about them to make the lie convincing.

### Spelling Check  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The least glamorous skill that saves seed phrases: wallets reject any word not spelled exactly as the list spells it.

### SPL Token  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
Solana's token standard, SPL being the equivalent of Ethereum's ERC. SPL tokens pay for their own accounts.

### Split Backup  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Splitting a phrase between locations or people so no single compromise reveals the whole secret.

### Spoof  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Falsifying a website, email address or token name to look legitimate. Fake Uniswap tokens showing up in your wallet are spoofs.

### Spot ETF  ·  TradFi  ·  Intermediate
An ETF that holds the actual underlying asset rather than futures contracts, meaning real coins are bought and locked in custody.

### Spread  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
The gap between the best bid and the best ask. Tight spread means a liquid, easy market.

### Stability Fee  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The interest paid on stablecoins you mint against collateral. It is the protocol's version of a central bank rate.

### Stablecoin  ·  Tokens  ·  Basic
A token designed to hold a steady value, usually one US dollar. Fiat-backed, crypto-backed and algorithmic versions each carry different risks.

### Stablecoin Dominance  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
Stablecoin market cap as a share of the whole market. Rising dominance usually means money is waiting on the sidelines.

### Stablecoin Rulebook  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The practical effect of the GENIUS Act - reserves, redemptions, disclosures and who may legally issue a dollar token in the US.

### Stablecoin Savings  ·  TradFi  ·  Intermediate
Holding dollars as tokens instead of in a local bank - the fastest-growing use of crypto in countries with high inflation.

### Stablecoin Supply  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
The total value of dollar tokens in circulation - one of the best measures of real capital sitting inside crypto.

### Stablecoin Yield Product  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
An app that pays a rate on dollar tokens, borrowing the reserve yield or lending the balance out. The risk lives in the details.

### Staking  ·  DeFi  ·  Basic
Locking up coins to help secure a proof-of-stake network in exchange for rewards. Usually 2-15% a year, depending on the chain.

### Staking Provider  ·  DeFi  ·  Intermediate
A service that runs validators for you, often taking a cut of the rewards. Centralized providers make a chain more centralised.

### Stale Price  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
An oracle price that has not updated when it should have, letting positions be liquidated or borrowed against unfairly.

### Starknet  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
A STARK-based zk-rollup using the Cairo language, founded by the team behind zk-STARKs.

### State  ·  Tech  ·  Intermediate
The complete current picture of who owns what and what every smart contract remembers. It is updated with every block.

### State Bloat  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The endless growth of the data a full node must store, one of the structural costs of permanent ledgers.

### State Channel  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
A way for two parties to transact privately off-chain and settle only the net result on-chain. Lightning is a network of these.

### State Channel Network  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Many two-party channels routed together, which is how Lightning moves payments between strangers.

### State Expiry  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A proposed fix for state bloat - letting untouched state lapse and be revived on demand.

### State Root  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The hash of the entire state of a blockchain at a given block, in Ethereum's case a Patricia Merkle trie.

### Stateless Client  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A client that verifies blocks without storing the whole state, using compact proofs instead. The endgame for running a node on a phone.

### Stealth Address  ·  Privacy  ·  Advanced
A fresh one-time address generated for each payment, so a receiver's public address never appears on-chain.

### Steel Backup  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Stamping your seed words into metal so fire and flood do not destroy your only copy.

### Stock-to-Flow  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
A popular (and widely criticised) Bitcoin model that treats scarcity like gold, comparing existing supply to new issuance.

### Stop-Loss  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
An order that triggers a sale once the price falls to a level you set, to cap your losses.

### Storage Proof  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A cryptographic proof that a specific value exists in a contract's storage, used by bridges and light clients.

### Storage Slot  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A 32-byte slot in a contract's persistent memory. Writing one costs real money, reading one is nearly free.

### Strategy  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The exact set of moves a vault performs with your money, including where it takes risk.

### Stuck Transaction  ·  Basics  ·  Advanced
A transaction that will not confirm because the fee was too low. Fix it by speeding up, replacing or cancelling it.

### Subgraph  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A Graph Protocol definition that tells an indexer which contract events matter and how to shape them into a queryable API.

### Subnet  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
A specialised competition inside Bittensor. Miners produce work, validators score it, and emissions flow to whoever performs best.

### Substrate  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The Rust framework Polkadot is built from, used to create app-specific chains that plug into shared security.

### Sui  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A Move-based chain using an object model instead of accounts, so independent transactions can be executed in parallel and confirmed in under a second.

### Superchain  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
Optimism's vision of many OP Stack chains sharing upgrades, governance and liquidity.

### Supercycle  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The theory that crypto no longer has violent boom-bust cycles because of institutional money. Marketed confidently every cycle.

### Superstate  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
A tokenised fund manager building on-chain treasuries and equity products designed to meet US securities rules.

### Supply  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
How many tokens exist. Circulating supply counts coins actually in the market; total supply counts minted; max supply counts the hard cap.

### Supply Cap  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
A hard ceiling on how much of an asset can be supplied to a market, limiting exposure to a risky collateral.

### Supply Chain Attack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Poisoning a library, an update or a build pipeline so malicious code ships inside trusted software.

### Supply Chain Compromise  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Malicious code shipped inside a trusted dependency or update, so the victim installs the attack themselves.

### SVM  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The Solana Virtual Machine, a parallel runtime that processes non-overlapping transactions simultaneously.

### Sweeper  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A bot that instantly empties any funds sent to a compromised or burned address.

### Swing Trading  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
Holding a position for days or weeks to catch a larger move.

### Sybil Attack  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
One attacker creates thousands of fake identities to gain outsized influence. Proof of work and proof of stake are defences against it.

### Sybil Hunter  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Someone finding and excluding fake multi-wallet accounts so airdrops go to real humans. A whole analytics sub-industry.

### Sybil Resistance  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The techniques projects use to tell real users from bot farms - proof of personhood, activity scoring, wallet age.

### Synthetic  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
A token that tracks the price of something else - a stock, a currency, an index - without holding the underlying asset.

### T+1 Settlement  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
The US moved to next-day settlement for securities in May 2024, tightening the window between trade and delivery. Crypto has no such window - settlement is minutes.

### Take Profit  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
An order that automatically sells once a target profit is reached.

### Taker  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
A trader who fills against an existing order. Takers pay the higher fee.

### Tamper-evident  ·  Basics  ·  Advanced
Any change to the data leaves a visible cryptographic fingerprint, so tampering is immediately detectable even if it is not prevented.

### TAO  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
Bittensor's token, paid to miners and validators who contribute to machine-learning subnets.

### Tap-to-Earn  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
Games where tapping the screen earns points that may become tokens. Enormous in 2024, and mostly abandoned within months.

### Taproot  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
Bitcoin's 2021 upgrade adding Schnorr signatures and better privacy, letting complex spend conditions look identical to ordinary payments on-chain.

### Taproot Assets  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
A protocol for issuing stablecoins and other real-world assets on Bitcoin while settling through Lightning.

### Tax Loss Harvesting  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Realising losses to offset gains, subject to each country's rules about repurchasing the same asset.

### Tax Lot  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
A specific tranche of an asset bought at a specific price. Choosing which lot to sell changes the tax you owe.

### Tax Reporting  ·  Regulation  ·  Intermediate
Reporting gains, income and staking rewards to your tax authority. In many countries, selling or spending crypto creates a taxable event.

### TEE Inference  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
Running AI models inside secure enclaves so the operator cannot see the prompt or tamper with the output.

### Telegram  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
Where most token communities live, and where most scams recruit.

### Telegram Mini App  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
A lightweight app running inside Telegram, with a wallet attached. In 2024-25 they became the biggest consumer crypto onboarding funnel in the world.

### Telegram Wallet  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
The custodial wallet built into Telegram, letting hundreds of millions of users hold and send USDT without knowing what a seed phrase is.

### Terra Collapse  ·  History  ·  Advanced
May 2022 - the algorithmic stablecoin UST depegged and LUNA hyperinflated to billions of tokens, wiping out roughly $40 billion in days and triggering the 2022 credit crisis.

### Testnet  ·  Basics  ·  Basic
A copy of a network used for testing where the coins are worthless, so developers can experiment without risking real funds.

### Tether  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
Issuer of USDT, the largest stablecoin, used overwhelmingly for trading and for saving dollars where local currency fails. It operates offshore and has paid large US fines.

### TGE  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Token Generation Event - the moment a token contract goes live and the supply is created. Often synonymous with launch day.

### The DAO  ·  History  ·  Advanced
A 2016 venture fund governed by token holders that raised $150 million and was drained of $60 million by a reentrancy attack, forcing Ethereum to hard fork into ETH and ETC.

### The Merge  ·  Ethereum  ·  Intermediate
September 2022, when Ethereum switched from mining to staking. It cut the network's energy use by about 99.95%.

### Three Arrows Capital  ·  History  ·  Advanced
3AC - the crypto hedge fund that blew up in 2022 as its Luna and staked-Ether positions unravelled, dragging down lenders across the market.

### Throughput  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The actual sustained capacity a network delivers under real conditions, as opposed to its theoretical maximum.

### Tick Size  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
The smallest price increment a market allows.

### Time to Finality  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
How long until a transaction cannot be reversed. Ethereum is about 13 minutes; Solana is measured in seconds.

### Timelock  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A deliberate delay between a governance decision and its execution, giving users time to exit if they dislike the change.

### Timelock (Security)  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
A delay on privileged actions so users can see a malicious upgrade coming and exit in time.

### Timestamp  ·  Basics  ·  Basic
The record of when a block or transaction was created, used for ordering events on-chain.

### To the Moon  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
The rallying cry for a violent, glorious upward move.

### Token  ·  Tokens  ·  Basic
A digital asset on a blockchain. It can represent anything - money, a share, a vote, a ticket, a piece of art, a receipt.

### Token Approval Drainer  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
See wallet drainer - the most common way people lose NFTs and stablecoins without ever revealing a seed phrase.

### Token Extensions  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Solana's upgradeable token standard, adding transfer fees, confidential transfers, required memos and other compliance-friendly features.

### Token Terminal  ·  Data  ·  Advanced
Financial statements for protocols - revenue, fees, earnings - presented like a public company's filings.

### Token URI  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The pointer inside an NFT contract that says where its metadata and image live.

### Tokenised Money Market Fund  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
A regulated fund whose shares trade as tokens — the fastest-growing bridge between traditional cash management and DeFi collateral.

### Tokenised Treasuries  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
US government bonds wrapped as tokens, letting DeFi users hold the risk-free rate on-chain. BlackRock's BUIDL and Franklin Templeton's BENJI led the way.

### Tokenomics  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
The economic design of a token - supply, distribution, emissions, burns and incentives. Most token failures are tokenomics failures.

### TON  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The Open Network, built by Telegram's founders, with Telegram Mini Apps turning its wallet into a mass-market consumer product.

### Tornado Cash  ·  Privacy  ·  Intermediate
The mixer used to break the on-chain link between deposit and withdrawal. Sanctioned by the US Treasury in 2022 and later unwound in court rulings.

### Total Market Cap  ·  Metrics  ·  Intermediate
The combined value of every crypto asset, and the headline number quoted by mainstream media when describing the market.

### TPS  ·  Tech  ·  Intermediate
Transactions per second. The most quoted and least meaningful metric in crypto, because real throughput depends on how heavy each transaction is.

### Trading Bot  ·  Trading  ·  Intermediate
Software that trades automatically on rules or signals, from simple grid bots to complex market-making systems.

### Trading Journal  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A record of why you entered, exited and how you felt. The cheapest way to improve and the least popular.

### TradingView  ·  Data  ·  Intermediate
The charting platform where almost every crypto trader draws support and resistance lines.

### Traits  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The individual attributes that make up an NFT - background, hat, eyes. Rarity is calculated from how often each trait appears.

### Transaction  ·  Basics  ·  Basic
A signed instruction to move value or call a contract, broadcast to the network and eventually sealed into a block.

### Transaction Simulation  ·  Security  ·  Intermediate
Wallets showing exactly what will change before you sign. The single most effective consumer protection in crypto.

### Travel Rule  ·  Compliance  ·  Advanced
The FATF requirement that exchanges pass sender and receiver details along with transfers above a threshold, just like banks do.

### Travel Rule Enforcement  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
When regulators start actually checking that exchanges pass sender and receiver data on transfers, not just promising to.

### TRC-20  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
Tron's token standard, widely used for cheap USDT transfers.

### Treasury Automation  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
Rules that sweep idle stablecoins into yield and back when obligations hit — CFO tooling built on-chain.

### Treasury Company  ·  TradFi  ·  Advanced
A public company that holds crypto as a reserve asset, following Strategy's model.

### Treasury Diversification  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
When a DAO converts part of its native token treasury into stablecoins or BTC, reducing its dependence on its own price.

### Treasury Management  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
How a DAO stewards its assets: runway, diversification, yield and approval workflows. Most DAOs are bad at it, which is why many die slowly.

### Trench Coat  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
Not a garment. The symbol of someone who spends all day in the trenches hunting tiny coins.

### Trenches  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
The messy world of brand-new, tiny market-cap tokens on launchpads and Telegram groups, where everyone is early and mostly wrong.

### Trezor  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The oldest hardware wallet brand, from SatoshiLabs, and one of the first real physical Bitcoin wallets.

### Triangular Arbitrage  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
Exploiting a mispricing across three trading pairs - for example BTC to ETH to USDT and back to BTC.

### Trust Wallet  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
The popular self-custody mobile wallet, and one of the most impersonated brand names in app stores.

### Trusted Execution Environment  ·  Privacy  ·  Advanced
A secure enclave in a chip that can run code without even the machine's owner seeing inside it.

### Trustless  ·  Basics  ·  Intermediate
A system where you do not have to trust a middleman, because the code and cryptography make cheating mathematically impractical.

### Truth Terminal  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
The AI agent that famously talked an internet figure into creating a memecoin, becoming the defining story of the agent era.

### Turkey Crypto Law  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The 2024 framework licensing crypto service providers under the Capital Markets Board - partly in response to record stablecoin usage.

### TVL  ·  Metrics  ·  Intermediate
Total Value Locked - the value of assets deposited in a protocol's contracts. The simplest gauge of how much people trust it with.

### TVL Dominance  ·  Metrics  ·  Advanced
A protocol's share of total value locked in its chain or sector, used to judge relative competitive position.

### TWAP  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
Time-Weighted Average Price - averaging a price over a window so a single manipulated candle cannot fool a protocol.

### TWAP Oracle  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
A price averaged over time so a single manipulated candle cannot fool a protocol. Slow, which is a feature.

### TXID  ·  Basics  ·  Intermediate
Transaction ID - the unique hash that identifies a transaction and lets you look it up on a block explorer.

### Unbanked  ·  TradFi  ·  Intermediate
People without access to banking. Roughly 1.4 billion adults - the population crypto payments genuinely serve, when the UX is good enough.

### Unbonding Period  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The cooldown between asking to unstake and actually getting your coins back - a few days on Ethereum, up to 21 days on Cosmos chains.

### Unconfirmed  ·  Basics  ·  Intermediate
A transaction sitting in the mempool that has not yet been mined into a block.

### Undercollateralized  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Loans that need no or little collateral, which requires identity, reputation or a trusted counterparty.

### Uniswap  ·  Names  ·  Intermediate
The largest decentralized exchange, and the protocol that made the automated market maker mainstream. Its 2024 UNI token, v4 upgrade and fee switch dominated DeFi talk.

### Unlock  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The date tokens become sellable. Big unlocks often mean selling pressure.

### Up Only  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A chant meaning this only goes up. Technically never true, emotionally essential.

A contract whose code can be swapped by an admin key. Powerful and convenient, but it means you are trusting whoever holds that key.

### US Spot Bitcoin ETF  ·  History  ·  Intermediate
Approved 10 January 2024 after a decade of rejections and one successful court appeal by Grayscale. It opened crypto to mainstream brokerage accounts and pension money.

### USDC  ·  Tokens  ·  Basic
Circle's dollar stablecoin, the compliance-friendly standard on Ethereum, Base and Solana.

### USDT  ·  Tokens  ·  Basic
Tether's dollar stablecoin, the most traded crypto asset in the world by volume, especially on non-US exchanges.

### UTXO  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
Unspent Transaction Output - Bitcoin's accounting model. Coins are like banknotes: you spend whole notes and receive change back.

### UTXO (Spending)  ·  Bitcoin  ·  Advanced
Bitcoin transactions consume whole previous outputs and create new ones. Your balance is simply the sum of the outputs you can spend.

### Validator  ·  Mining  ·  Intermediate
A node that locks up stake to propose and attest to blocks. Validators replace miners on proof-of-stake chains.

### Validator Client  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
The proof-of-stake side of an Ethereum node: it signs attestations and block proposals, and holds the validator key.

### Validity Proof  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
Proof that a batch of transactions is correct, verified on-chain before the state update is accepted.

### Validium  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
A zk system that proves execution but keeps data off-chain, which makes it cheaper and trust assumptions higher.

Launching a clone of a protocol with better incentives to drain its users and liquidity. SushiSwap did it to Uniswap in 2020.

### Vanity Address  ·  Security  ·  Advanced

### Vaporware  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced
A project that exists only in announcements.

### VARA  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority, the world's first dedicated crypto regulator, licensing exchanges, custodians and brokers in the emirate.

### VASP  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Virtual Asset Service Provider - the international term for a business that exchanges or custodies crypto. Most countries license VASPs.

### VASP Licence  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The licence crypto businesses need in Nigeria and dozens of other countries to operate legally - the local version of MiCA compliance.

### VASP Registration  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
The lighter-touch route many developing markets use, requiring only registration and AML reporting rather than a full securities licence.

### Vault  ·  DeFi  ·  Intermediate
A contract that pools user deposits and runs a strategy on them, from automated yield to structured products.

### Vault Hygiene  ·  Security  ·  Advanced
Reviewing saved words, revoked approvals and stale keys on a schedule. Boring maintenance that prevents real losses.

### Vault Manager  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The role that runs or oversees a DeFi vault's strategy and risk parameters.

### Vault Strategy Migration  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
Moving depositor funds from one yield strategy to another, usually triggered by deteriorating returns or risk.

### Verifiable Inference  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
Proving that an AI model really ran the prompt you paid for, instead of a cheaper model faking the answer.

### Verkle Tree  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A planned data structure that replaces Ethereum's Merkle-Patricia trie with far smaller proofs, making stateless clients practical.

### Vesting  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate
Releasing team and investor tokens gradually over months or years so they cannot dump everything on day one. Cliffs are the initial lock-up.

### veToken  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
A vote-escrowed token you receive for locking your tokens for months or years, granting boosted rewards and governance power.

### Virtual Asset Law  ·  Regulation  ·  Advanced
Korea's 2024 framework requiring exchanges to segregate customer assets and carry insurance - passed after the Terra collapse hit Korean households hardest.

### Virtual Land  ·  Web3  ·  Advanced
Tokenised plots inside a virtual world, minted as NFTs. Sold for fortunes in 2021 and much less since.

### Virtuals  ·  AI x Crypto  ·  Advanced
The Base-based launch platform for AI agents, each with its own token, wallet and revenue.

### Vitalik Buterin  ·  Names  ·  Basic
Ethereum's co-founder and most influential thinker, who proposed the platform at 19 and still shapes its roadmap.

### Volatility Smile  ·  Trading  ·  Advanced
The pattern where out-of-the-money options trade at higher implied volatility than at-the-money ones, reflecting fear of tail moves.

Total value traded over a period. Real volume means real interest; fake volume means market makers paid to look busy.

Paying for delegated votes, openly in some protocols, quietly in others. Legal in most jurisdictions and corrosive everywhere.

### Vote Escrow  ·  DeFi  ·  Advanced
The lock-up mechanism behind veTokens - the longer you lock, the more voting power and yield share you receive.

### Vyper  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A Python-flavoured smart contract language built for security and readability, deliberately stripped of Solidity's trickier features.

### WAGMI  ·  Culture  ·  Intermediate
We're All Gonna Make It - an optimistic rallying cry in group chats and replies.

Browser-tooling categories now built into wallets that warn you before you sign something dangerous.

### Wojak  ·  Culture  ·  Advanced

Worldcoin's iris-based proof of personhood, used to verify humanness in apps and airdrops.

### XRP  ·  Tokens  ·  Intermediate

### Yield  ·  DeFi  ·  Intermediate
The return you earn for supplying capital or taking risk, quoted as an APR or APY.

### Yield Farming  ·  DeFi  ·  Intermediate
Moving capital between protocols to chase the best returns, often harvesting token rewards along the way. Also called liquidity mining.

### Yield-Bearing Stablecoin  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
A dollar stablecoin that also pays you interest, like Ethena's sUSDe or Mountain's USDM, by passing on the yield of the reserves behind it.

### Zcash  ·  Tokens  ·  Advanced
The zk-SNARK privacy coin with optional shielded transactions, and one of the most rigorous cryptographic codebases in crypto.

### Zero-Knowledge Proof  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A cryptographic method that proves you know something is true without revealing what it is. The foundation of privacy and scaling tech.

### ZK Compression  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
Storing Solana state as compressed Merkle data instead of full accounts, cutting the cost of large NFT collections and airdrops by orders of magnitude.

### ZK Rollup  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
A rollup that proves every batch correct with a zero-knowledge proof before it settles, so withdrawals need no challenge period. zkSync, Starknet and Scroll use this.

### ZK-KYC  ·  Privacy  ·  Advanced
Proving you passed a KYC check or meet a threshold without revealing your name or documents.

### zk-SNARK  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A small, fast-to-verify zero-knowledge proof. It needs a trusted setup and is the workhorse of privacy coins and many zk-rollups.

### zk-STARK  ·  Tech  ·  Advanced
A transparent zero-knowledge proof - no trusted setup required and quantum-resistant, but the proofs are much bigger.

### zkEVM  ·  Scaling  ·  Advanced
An EVM that runs inside a zero-knowledge proof, letting ordinary Ethereum contracts benefit from zk scaling.

### zkSync  ·  Names  ·  Advanced
A zk-rollup on Ethereum and one of the earliest to bring zero-knowledge proofs to general-purpose contracts.
